03 / Research

Studying how AI systems can improve their own instructions.

My current independent research implementation combines evolutionary search, reflective prompt optimization, and quality-diversity methods.

GEPA × Promptbreeder × MAP-Elites

A nested prompt optimizer for robust text-cleanup behavior.

The outer loop evolves the reflection prompts that guide GEPA. Each candidate then starts a fresh inner optimization run, and MAP-Elites archives strong meta-prompts across different ambiguity and noise-handling strategies.

Explore the research repo ↗
  1. 01
    Evolve

    Promptbreeder mutates the meta-prompt that governs reflection.

  2. 02
    Optimize

    GEPA runs in isolation to improve the downstream task prompt.

  3. 03
    Evaluate

    Held-out probes measure ambiguity, noise, and meaning preservation.

  4. 04
    Archive

    MAP-Elites keeps diverse high-quality strategies across 16 niches.

Why this interests me

I’m drawn to research that makes model behavior more reliable, explainable, and useful—not just more impressive in a demo. This project is a starting point for deeper work in optimization, evaluation, and adaptive AI systems.
