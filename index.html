03 / Research

Teaching reflection prompts to resolve ambiguity.

As an undergraduate AI/ML researcher, I’m studying prompt evolution, LLM evaluation, evolutionary memory, and quality-diversity methods.

Undergraduate AI/ML Researcher · 2026–Present

Meta-GEPA: self-referential reflection tuning for conversational ambiguity.

I’m designing a prompt-evolution framework that uses LLM evaluator feedback and self-referential reflection to improve ambiguity resolution—without model fine-tuning or forcing a clarification turn.

Explore the research repo ↗
  1. 01
    Evolve

    Reflection prompts adapt from evaluator feedback and self-referential tuning.

  2. 02
    Benchmark

    A 20-case benchmark spans lexical, syntactic, referential, semantic, and pragmatic ambiguity.

  3. 03
    Evaluate

    Heuristic evaluation measures accuracy, generalization, and prompt efficiency.

  4. 04
    Preserve

    MAP-Elites and multi-objective selection retain specialist prompts instead of collapsing to one strategy.

Why this interests me

I’m drawn to research that makes model behavior more reliable, explainable, and useful—not just more impressive in a demo. Meta-GEPA lets me investigate how feedback, memory, and evolutionary search can improve model behavior without changing the underlying model.
